## What "color prediction" actually means in Pakistan

A **colour-prediction app** is a short-session game where you bet on which colour (typically **Red**, **Green**, or **Violet**) will land at the end of a 30-second to 3-minute round. Outcomes are server-decided and displayed as a number (0–9) mapped to a colour.

Searchers in PK often bundle this with **roulette wheels**, **Aviator-style crash games**, and **binary up/down predictions** under one label, but the maths and risk profile differ:

| Format | Typical round | Payout style | Where you'll see it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Red / Green binary | 30s–60s | ~1.95x on win | Most PK colour apps |
| Red / Green / Violet | 1–3 min | 2x on R or G, ~4.5x on Violet | K99-style apps |
| Roulette wheel | 1 min | Number-dependent multiplier | Casino-style hybrids |
| Crash / Aviator | 5–60s | Cash out before crash | WC99, 89F-style |

## The payout math nobody else shows you

Take a Red/Green/Violet round. Marketing says "2x on Red". Maths says:

- 10 numbers (0–9). Red = 5 numbers, Green = 4 numbers, Violet = 1 number.
- Red wins ~50% but pays 2x → expected return per PKR 100 bet is PKR 100 (looks fair).
- **Catch:** half the "Red" numbers (e.g. 0) are also tagged Violet, so a "Red" bet on 0 pays only 1.5x. That hidden split is the **house edge** (~3–5%).
- Over 1,000 rounds at PKR 100 each, expected loss is roughly **PKR 3,000–5,000**.

If a competitor page tells you "predict colour, double your money," they are skipping this maths. We're not.

## 10-point safety checklist before you install a colour-prediction APK

1. Publisher domain has a real About + contact email (not just Telegram).
2. APK file size and version match what the listing states.
3. Mirror link is HTTPS and not a Telegram-only drop.
4. JazzCash / EasyPaisa support is shown **inside the app**, not just on the landing page.
5. Withdrawal minimum is stated in PKR, not "low".
6. KYC happens inside the app — never via WhatsApp agent.
7. No request for SMS, Accessibility, or Device Admin permissions.
8. Bonus terms disclose the wagering ratio (e.g. 30x rollover) before you deposit.
9. No "deposit a fee to release your withdrawal" pattern.
10. The page tells you the **house edge** exists.

If 3+ items fail, treat the APK as high-risk. Cross-read [Fake casino apps in Pakistan](/guides/fake-casino-apps-pakistan).
