## What "no investment" often means in the wild

Competitors like pakgame.app and sixgame.cloud advertise **PKR 5,000 welcome bonus, no deposit required**. Here's what that actually unlocks:

| Marketing phrase | What's almost always hidden |
| --- | --- |
| "Free PKR 88 signup bonus" | 20–50x wagering rollover before any withdrawal |
| "PKR 5,000 welcome bonus" | Bonus is play-money credit, not withdrawable PKR |
| "No deposit withdrawal" | Hard cap (e.g. PKR 50) until first deposit |
| "Daily free spin" | Payout capped at PKR 5–20 unless you top up |
| "Refer a friend, earn PKR 600" | Friend must deposit + complete wagering for you to earn |
| "Task-based earning" | PKR 1–3 per task; threshold to cash out is PKR 200–500 |

Forum screenshots showing "I withdrew PKR 50,000 with no deposit" are **almost always referral funnels** — the screenshot poster earned via your eventual deposit, not theirs.

## What you can actually withdraw without depositing

Honest math, based on the patterns we see across PK earning apps:

- **Pure signup bonus**: PKR 0–88 cashable after CNIC KYC + phone OTP, often gated by a tiny first deposit.
- **Daily login + task chains**: PKR 5–30 per day if you grind 30–60 minutes (data + ad attention is your "investment").
- **Referrals**: realistic PKR 100–500 per active referral, *if* they deposit. Without deposit, often PKR 0–20.
- **Pure prediction play with bonus credit**: expected value is negative (house edge); the bonus is a marketing line, not free PKR.

Realistic ceiling without ever depositing your own PKR: ~PKR 500–2,000 per month, requiring 1–2 hours daily and a working referral network. That's below minimum wage in Pakistan.
